## Most Important Definitions
> [!Defintions] [[Categories]]
> A category is a:
> - Collection of objects: $A$, $B$, $C$ etc. 
> - A collections of morphisms: $f$, $g$, ...
> 	- for every objects there exists an identity morphism $\mathcal 1_a$
> 	- Morphisms may compose if domain and codomain match.
